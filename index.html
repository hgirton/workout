Round 1

Working
circuit.

All 6 back to back. Rest 60–90 sec after. Then Round 2.

1
Goblet Squat
10lb KB at chest. Feet hip-width, toes out. Sit back and down — chest up. Drive through heels to stand.
10 reps · 10lb
2
Band Pull-Apart
Arms straight at shoulder height. Pull band apart squeezing shoulder blades. Control the return.
15 reps · medium
3
Single-Leg Deadlift (Assisted)
One hand on wall. 10lb KB opposite hand. Hinge at hip — torso tips forward, free leg floats back. Return.
8 each side · 10lb
4
Kettlebell Deadlift
15lb KB on floor. Hinge, grip, stand by squeezing glutes and driving hips forward. Lower with control.
10 reps · 15lb
5
Tall Kneeling Press
Both knees on mat. 2lb weights or light band. Press overhead. If back arches — too heavy.
12 reps · light
6
Dead Bug
Back down, arms up, shins parallel. Lower opposite arm + leg. Ribs stay stapled to floor. Slow is hard.
8 each side

Round 2 · Push More

Same
circuit.

You know what you're doing now. A little more on each one.

1
Goblet Squat
Go a little lower. Pause at the bottom for a beat before driving up.
10 reps · 10lb
2
Band Pull-Apart
Slower on the return. Feel the squeeze between your shoulder blades.
15 reps
3
Single-Leg Deadlift
Try lightening the wall touch — fingertips only if you can.
8 each side · 10lb
4
Kettlebell Deadlift
Slower on the way down. 3-count lower. This is the most important one.
10 reps · 15lb
5
Tall Kneeling Press
Stay tall. Don't let your ribs flare on the press up.
12 reps
6
Dead Bug
Even slower. Add a 2-second hold at the bottom if it still feels easy.
8 each side

Cool-Down · 8 min

Let it
settle.

You did the work. Now let your nervous system downshift.

1
Standing Figure-Four Stretch
Cross ankle over opposite knee. Bend into standing leg. Feel the outer hip. Hold 30 sec each side.
30 sec each
2
Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor
One knee on mat, one foot forward. Tuck pelvis, lean forward gently. Breathe into the front of the back hip.
30 sec each
3
Supine Hamstring Stretch
On your back. Band around one foot, straighten leg toward ceiling. Other leg flat. Pull gently.
30 sec each
4
Child's Pose
Kneel, hips back toward heels, arms long. Forehead down. Just breathe. You earned this minute.
60 seconds
